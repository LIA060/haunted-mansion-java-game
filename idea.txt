The player must investigate the haunted mansion in the game and figure out how to get out before it gets too dangerous. When the player first arrives in R0 (entrance),  a terrified servant warns them about the dangers inside the mansion and says they need a key to escape. R1 (library) has old books, dusty shelves and a journal with clues about the mansion, as well as a torch that the player can take. R2 (dining room) has a long dining table and vintage furniture, with strange scary noises. The player must spend time in this optional chamber, which has no important items or NPCs. Paintings of the mansions previous residents are in  R3 (portrait gallery), along with a ghost NPC who can give the player a clue about the key's location. The antique bed and wardrobe in R4 (old bedroom) are where the escape key is hidden. The sealed chamber, R5( secret escape tunnel), can only be entered using the key found in R4. Once the player finds it, they win by unlocking R5 and escaping the mansion.
